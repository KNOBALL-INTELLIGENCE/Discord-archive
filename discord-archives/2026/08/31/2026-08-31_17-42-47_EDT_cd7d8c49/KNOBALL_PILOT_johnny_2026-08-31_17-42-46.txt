> Handoff for John — Vizard Credit Model → Possible KnoBall Pricing Model
> 
> Context: We tested Vizard as a way to take full 45–60 minute YouTube/game videos and automatically find touchdowns, turnovers, big plays, and other highlights before sending the best clips into CapCut.
> 
> What Vizard does
> 
> * Standard AI processing is roughly 1 credit per 1 minute of source video.
> * A 54-minute game uses about 54 credits.
> * Credits are charged for processing the source, not per individual short produced.
> * One full game can therefore produce multiple candidate clips from one credit charge.
> * Paid plans provide monthly credit pools, with larger tiers getting more processing capacity.
> 
> Why this matters for KnoBall
> 
> Vizard is a clean example of the pricing structure we have already discussed for KnoBall:
> 
> Subscription tier + included usage credits + optional additional credits
> 
> Instead of making every AI feature unlimited inside a flat subscription, KnoBall could eventually separate:
> 
> Tier = access and permissions
> Credits = expensive AI / compute usage
> 
> Potential credit-consuming KnoBall actions could include:
> 
> * full-game AI analysis
> * video/highlight processing
> * automated scouting reports
> * advanced simulations
> * AI-generated league/media content
> * large data/report jobs
> * premium intelligence operations
